About

Christian Wilson is a composer based out of Utah. Ever since he was little, he has a passion for musical storytelling. Being raised with games like The Legend of Zelda and Final Fantasy, he has strived to emulate the musical highlights of the video game industry. While he has only worked on two projects, he is hopeful that his music will make a profound impact among gamers and non-gamers alike.
